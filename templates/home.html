Key Pillars of the Grievance Cell

Designed in compliance with statutory institutional guidelines to ensure transparency, confidential protection, and speedy facility maintenance.

100% Anonymity

Students' identities are completely isolated from department administrators. Redressal officers view only the ticket details and issue category, eliminating prejudice.

Duplicate Prevention

Intelligent AI similarity detection prevents redundant tickets. Students can directly "Support" existing issues, elevating their priority to administrative attention.

Executive SLA Escalations

High-priority tickets unresolved within 3 days automatically escalate straight to the Principal's executive desk for urgent administrative intervention.

Automated Email Dispatch

Integrated notification engine dispatches tracking confirmations to students and immediate assignment tickets to department heads via institutional email.

Milestone Timeline

Full audit trail from initial submission, inspection review, technician dispatch, to official remark closure with time-stamped status transitions.

Official Notices & Bulletins

Centralized campus circulars regarding scheduled facility repairs, power/water shutdowns, and academic facility upgrades issued by the Principal.

How Grievance Redressal Operates

Four transparent stages from registration to verified resolution.

1
Roll Number Check

Student account registration verified securely against the college's pre-approved database.

2
File Grievance

Submit issue details with category & priority. Smart duplicate checks guide support voting.

3
Admin Resolution

Maintenance team investigates, records official replies, and updates status in real-time.

4
Closed & Notified

Automated email dispatched upon resolution, and the grievance timeline is permanently logged.
