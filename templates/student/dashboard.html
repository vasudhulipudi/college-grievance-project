Student Redressal Guidelines
Official
  • Confidentiality Protection: Your name and roll number are never shown to department administrators or technicians resolving your complaint.
  • Duplicate Issue Voting: When submitting an issue, if an identical active ticket already exists, you can click "Support Existing Complaint" to upvote its urgency instead of creating redundant tickets.
  • SLA Escalation Guarantee: If high-priority issues are not addressed within the designated timeframe, they are automatically escalated to the Principal's executive desk.
  • Status Notifications: Whenever an administrator responds or changes your complaint's progress, you will receive an automatic institutional confirmation email.
